Reverse Engineering HeyGen: Architecture & Core Principles

1. Text-to-Speech & Acoustic Prosody Decomposition

HeyGen does not generate video directly from raw text. The text is first synthesized into neural audio waveforms. The pipeline extracts:

  • Phonemes: Acoustic segments that determine mouth shape (bilabial plosives, fricatives, open/round vowels).
  • Prosody & Pitch (F0): Fundamental frequency contour and RMS energy envelope. Rising pitch and volume peaks correspond to emphasized words and question cadence.

2. Audio-Driven Lip Sync & Micro-Expressions

Rather than simple 2D image swaps, HeyGen utilizes 3DMM / FLAME / neural mesh deformation:

  • Viseme Morphing: Dynamically morphs jaw drop, lip spread (smiling), lip rounding (pucker), and oral cavity depth with spline co-articulation smoothing.
  • Natural Eye Blinking: Synthesizes non-linear eyelid curves (fast dip, smooth ease-out) timed every 3–4 seconds and snapping to speech pause boundaries.
  • Eyebrow & Gaze Dynamics: Eyebrows raise 4–8px in response to pitch/volume peaks. Subtle micro-saccades prevent the "dead eyes" uncanny valley.

3. Hand Gestures & Kinematic State Machine

Human conversational gestures are not random; they follow McNeill's gesture dimensions:

  • Preparation Phase (0.3–0.5s): Hand ascends smoothly from resting position towards presentation space with cubic easing.
  • Stroke / Apex Phase (0.5–0.8s): The peak of the gesture locks with the spoken word's acoustic stress peak (open palms, pointing, framing).
  • Retraction Phase (0.4–0.6s): Smooth recovery back to resting posture or transition to the next beat.

4. Multi-Layer Neural Rendering & Compositing

The compositing engine layers Background → Torso/Clothing → Articulated Hands → Dynamic Face Mesh → Lighting Alignment → H.264/AAC MP4 video with frame-accurate synchronization.
